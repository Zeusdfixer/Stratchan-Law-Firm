Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a divorce typically take?

Uncontested divorces can be finalised in as little as 4–6 months. Contested matters involving financial or custody disputes may take 12–24 months depending on complexity.

Will my case go to court?

We always explore negotiation, mediation and collaborative law first. Many matters are resolved without a court hearing, saving time, cost and emotional stress.

How are children's interests protected?

The welfare of children is paramount in all proceedings. We work to achieve arrangements that serve their best interests while respecting your parental rights.

Can you handle international family law matters?

Yes. We have experience in cross-jurisdictional family matters including international child abduction under the Hague Convention and overseas asset recovery.
