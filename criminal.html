Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if I am arrested?

Exercise your right to remain silent and immediately request legal representation. Do not answer questions without counsel present. Contact our chambers as soon as possible.

How do you approach criminal defence?

We begin with an immediate and thorough review of all evidence. We challenge every element of the prosecution case — from the legality of the investigation to the reliability of witness evidence.

Do you handle high-profile or sensitive cases?

Yes. Our chambers is experienced in managing cases with significant public attention while protecting client privacy and reputation throughout proceedings.

What are your success rates in criminal matters?

We maintain a strong record of acquittals and favourable outcomes. Our approach focuses on early intervention and meticulous case preparation.
